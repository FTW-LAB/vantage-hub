# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **ops@ftwlab.com** with:

- Affected repository and version/commit
- Impact assessment
- Reproduction steps (legal, public or authorized targets only)
- Optional fix suggestion

Do **not** open a public issue for unfixed security problems.

## Scope

- FTW Lab open repositories under the `ftwforge` organization
- Public site at https://ftwlab.com

## Out of scope

- Social engineering against non-consenting third parties
- Attacks on systems you do not own or lack written authorization to test
- Use of non-public data, credentials, or exploit PoCs against production systems

## Legal-use only

All research and tooling use must comply with the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
in `AUP.md` and applicable law. FTW Lab maintains a **legal OSINT / public-source**
posture only.
