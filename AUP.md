# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Product house:** FTW Lab  
**Contact:** ops@ftwlab.com  
**Ops:** ops.com  

## Allowed

- Use of published open-source code under its license
- Collection and analysis of **publicly available** information (legal OSINT)
- Security research against systems you own or have explicit written authorization to test
- Integration with **TARX upstream** packages and documented public APIs only

## Prohibited

- Unauthorized access to computer systems, accounts, or data
- Distribution of malware, ransomware, or exploit weaponization against third parties
- Scraping or processing personal data beyond what is lawfully public
- Misrepresenting FTW Lab affiliation
- Rebranding proprietary TARX product surfaces as FTW Lab product

## Enforcement

Violations may result in denial of support, repository bans, and referral to
appropriate authorities where required by law.
